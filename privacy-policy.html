Bookmark Glass

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 30, 2026
Local-First Storage No User Tracking No Account Required Open & Transparent

1. Overview & Core Philosophy

Bookmark Glass is designed with privacy-first principles. We believe that your bookmarks, personal reading list, and web habits belong strictly to you.

The extension operates primarily offline on your device. We do not maintain any user databases, cloud servers, or authentication systems. You do not need to create an account or provide an email address to use Bookmark Glass.

2. What Data Is Stored Locally on Your Device

All data created or organized in Bookmark Glass is saved locally in your browser:

Data Category Storage Mechanism Purpose
Browser Bookmarks browser.bookmarks / chrome.bookmarks Syncs directly with your browser's native bookmark and folder system.
Personal Library Links chrome.storage.local (key: my_library_links) Stores saved links, custom titles, tags, notes, and favorite flags.
Articles & Reading List chrome.storage.local (key: my_articles_library) Stores articles with reading statuses (Want to Read, Reading, Completed) and reading notes.
Folders & Visual Metadata chrome.storage.local (keys: custom_library_folders, folder_visual_metadata) Stores custom library folder hierarchies and visual folder color selections.
User Settings & Preferences chrome.storage.local (key: bookmark_glass_settings) Stores layout preferences (grid vs. list), theme preference (dark/light), and display options.
Recent Tags Cache localStorage (key: bg-tagpool) Stores a temporary pool of recently typed tags for autocomplete convenience.

3. External Requests & Third-Party Services

Bookmark Glass does not transmit your personal data, saved notes, or reading history to any proprietary servers. The extension only interacts with external services in the following specific circumstances:

Google Favicon Service:

To display website icons (favicons) next to your saved bookmarks and reading items, the extension loads images using the public Google Favicon endpoint:
https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=[domain]&sz=64

  • What is transmitted: Only the web domain name or origin (for example, github.com or wikipedia.org) of the saved link is included in the URL to fetch its icon.
  • What is NOT transmitted: Full page URLs, pathnames, search query strings, user notes, tags, or personal identifiers are never sent.
  • Provider: Google LLC. Requests are subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Voluntary Feedback Form: The extension dashboard includes a feedback banner linking to a Google Form. Clicking "Share Feedback" opens the form in a new tab. Submitting feedback is completely optional and handled directly by Google Forms.

No Telemetry or Tracking: We do not integrate Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Sentry, or any third-party telemetry, tracking, or advertising scripts.

4. Mozilla Firefox Data Collection Declarations

Under Mozilla's built-in consent framework for Firefox extensions, Bookmark Glass declares the following data collection permissions in manifest.json:

  • Bookmarks (bookmarksInfo): Declared because domain information from your saved bookmarks is queried against the Google Favicon service to display icons.
  • Browsing Activity (browsingActivity): Declared because when you open the popup to save your current active tab, the active page domain is resolved to fetch its favicon preview.

Neither of these data types is ever collected, logged, or retained on any external server managed by Bookmark Glass.

5. Browser Permissions Used

Permission Why It Is Needed
bookmarks To read existing browser bookmarks and save new bookmarks to your native bookmark folders.
storage To store your personal library links, reading list articles, and layout settings locally.
contextMenus To provide convenient right-click options ("Save to Bookmark Glass", "Save to My Library").
activeTab To obtain the active tab's title and URL strictly when you click the extension action or trigger a save.
scripting To inspect the active page's metadata (description and OpenGraph image) when initiating a save.

6. Data Retention, Control & Deletion

  • Individual Items: You can edit or delete any saved bookmark, library link, article, tag, or folder at any time directly through the Bookmark Glass interface.
  • Complete Erasure: To delete all data associated with Bookmark Glass, simply uninstall the extension from your browser (via about:addons in Firefox or edge://extensions in Edge). Uninstalling immediately purges all local storage.
